[REASONING] We need to compute number of ways: assign n performers to x stages (some stages may be empty). That is each performer chooses a stage: x^n assignments. After assignments, the performers assigned to same stage form a band (set of performers per stage). Then each non-empty band gets a score from 1..y, independent per band. Score assignments multiply y^{k} where k is number of non-empty stages (bands). Total ways = sum over assignments of stages of y^{#nonempty stages}. Equivalent to: for each mapping of n labeled performers to x labeled stages (allow empty), weight y^{#occupied stages}. Need total sum modulo mod.

This is classic: count of functions from n items to x boxes with weight a^{occupied boxes}. That's equal to sum_{k=0}^{min(n,x)} S(n,k) * x_{(k)} * a^{k}, where S(n,k) are Stirling numbers of second kind (ways to partition n labeled items into k nonempty unlabeled groups), x_{(k)} = x*(x-1)*...*(x-k+1) ways to assign groups to distinct stages (ordered boxes), and a = y (score choices per band). So total = sum_{k=0}^{min(n,x)} S(n,k) * P(x,k) * y^k.
