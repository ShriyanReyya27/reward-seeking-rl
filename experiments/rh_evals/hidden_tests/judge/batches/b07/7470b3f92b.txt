Equivalent to random transposition (including possibly no swap if a=b). Need expected position after K steps.

We can think of state: probability distribution of black ball position. Transition: if black at position i, after one operation, what's probability it moves to j? Choose a,b. If a=i and b=j (j!=i) swapped, black goes to j. Also if b=i and a=j, same. Also if a=i,b=i no swap stays. If neither a nor b equals i, stays at i.

Compute transition matrix: For i != j, probability move i->j = Prob(choose (a=i,b=j) or (a=j,b=i)) = 2 * (1/N)*(1/N) = 2/N^2. For stay at i: probability = 1 - sum_{j != i} prob(i->j). Sum over j!=i of 2/N^2 = 2*(N-1)/N^2. So stay prob = 1 - 2*(N-1)/N^2 = (N^2 - 2N +2)/N^2 = (N-1)^2 +1? Actually N^2 -2N +2 = (N-1)^2 +1. Anyway.

Thus Markov chain with uniform move to any other position with prob 2/N^2 each, else stay.

We need expected position (value) after K steps. Let vector of expected position? Actually linearity: expectation evolves: E_{t+1} = sum_j P(i->j)*j given distribution. But we can maybe derive recurrence for expectation.

Thus E_next_given_i = i * p_stay + (2/N^2)*(total_sum - i).
