The solution maintains an array `dp` of size N, where `dp[i]` holds the number of ways to be at the vertex corresponding to index i (adjusted by a rotation offset).  
At each of the K steps we:

1. Rotate the array implicitly by adjusting an offset (the cycle edge).  
2. For each extra edge (u→v) add the current number of ways at u to the destination v after the rotation.

Since M ≤ 50, this runs in O(K·M) ≤ 10⁷ operations, easily handling the limits.  
After K steps we sum all entries (mod 998244353) to obtain the total number of length‑K paths.
